What building my own list taught me

A while back I built an email list from zero to launch a paid guide I'd written. No existing audience, no paid ads. Just a lead magnet, a signup form, and a plan to earn attention before asking for anything.

I mapped a five week nurture sequence to move people from just joined to ready to buy, then set the whole thing to run on its own. The list itself was never really the point. The system behind it was, the same cleanup, segmentation, and sequencing logic that turns a few thousand quiet contacts into a list that actually gets used.
